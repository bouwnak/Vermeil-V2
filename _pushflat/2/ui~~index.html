          Internet & calendar

          Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Vermeil copies your events into the Planner every 30 minutes (read-only).

          Let your companion ADD events to Google Calendar too (one-time setup, ~5 min)
          1. Open console.cloud.google.com → create a project (e.g. “Vermeil”).
          2. APIs & Services → Library → “Google Calendar API” → Enable.
          3. OAuth consent screen → External → app name + your email → add yourself as a Test user.
          4. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → Desktop app → Download JSON.
          5. Here: choose that file, then Connect. Google asks you once.

          Needs once: pip install google-api-python-client google-auth-oauthlib

          This computer

          Vermeil looks at this computer and picks how hard to work. Low = for older or small laptops: a lighter voice, no voice clone, the small speech model and a simpler 3D room, so everything stays quick. On Auto a laptop on a low battery switches to Low by itself and back when you plug in. Say “low power mode”, “full power” or “scan my PC”.

          This PC

          Open this panel to see your PC's status.
          • Ctrl+Alt+V voice on/off
          • Ctrl+Alt+Space bring Vermeil up and type
          • Ctrl+Alt+H hide/show Vermeil
          • Ctrl+Alt+S explain what's on my screen
          • Ctrl+Alt+Esc emergency stop (speech, music, timers, macros, changes)

          Macros

          Repeatable lists of steps. Make one by saying, e.g. “make a macro called study mode: open my notes, play lofi, set volume to 30”.
